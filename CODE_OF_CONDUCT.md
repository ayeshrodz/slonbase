# Code of conduct

slonbase follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults,
personal or political attacks, and publishing other people's private information aren't
acceptable, here or in any space where you represent the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer,
[@ayeshrodz](https://github.com/ayeshrodz), through the contact details on their GitHub profile.
Reports are handled privately and promptly. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues,
and may ban contributors, for behavior that breaks this code, as described in the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
