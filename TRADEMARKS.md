## You can

- Say you use, host or build on slonbase, and link to it.
- Describe your product accurately: "built with slonbase", "compatible with slonbase", "a slonbase
  hosting service".
- Redistribute unmodified slonbase releases, packages and images under the name slonbase.
- Use the name and logo in articles, talks, tutorials and reviews about slonbase.

## Please don't

- Suggest that the maintainer or the slonbase project made, endorses or supports your product when
  it didn't.
- Name a fork, a modified build, a company, a product or a domain in a way that could be confused
  with slonbase. If you change the code and distribute it, give your version its own name.
- Use the logo as your own product's logo.

## Questions

If you are unsure, open an issue or write to the maintainer first. This is a short statement of
intent, not legal advice, and it may be extended as the project grows.
